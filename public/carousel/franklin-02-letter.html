What the Letter Proposes
Two Options.
One Conversation.
Franklin County Manager Ryan Preble outlined two paths forward for KLRWS partnership. The letter was sent to the city managers of Henderson, Oxford, and Warren County on May 21, 2026.
Option 1
51% ownership stake
At fair market value. A negotiating position, not a realistic outcome.
Option 2
Lower stake + expansion rights
Ownership proportional to investment. Franklin pays for its own infrastructure.
Also sent to: General Assembly members, Granville County, SGWASA, attorneys, Henderson Dispatch, Warren Dispatch, Franklin Times, WRAL
